A Better Way to Match Jobs

A job board where both candidates and employers stake money to participate. The mechanism creates costly signals that improve matching quality.

Current Job Market Problems

Zero-Cost Applications

Candidates spray applications everywhere at no cost, overwhelming employers with unqualified applicants.

AI Collapsed Signals

AI-written cover letters and resumes have made traditional screening signals worthless.

Adverse Selection

Best candidates are selective, worst apply everywhere. No way to signal genuine interest.

Vague Job Descriptions

Employers write vague descriptions to maximize optionality, making self-selection impossible.

The Solution: Skin in the Game

Candidates Stake Money

Pay a small fee (0.025% of salary) to apply. Well-calibrated candidates get it back plus a bonus.

Employers Stake Money

Stake 2.5% of salary. Efficient hiring returns your stake plus bonus. Inefficient? You lose some.

Self-Calibrating System

Miscalibration costs money. The system naturally filters for serious, well-matched participants.

Iterated Learning

Both sides learn from outcomes. Bad actors exit, good actors thrive.

How Money Flows

The platform creates a bilateral market where well-calibrated participants are rewarded and miscalibrated ones subsidize them.

Candidates who accurately self-assess where they're likely to succeed have positive expected value. Those who spray applications everywhere have negative expected value and eventually stop using the platform.

The Iterated Learning Game

Over multiple rounds, both candidates and employers learn their true market position.

Candidate Learning

Round 1: Apply to 10 jobs, succeed at 1. Lose money.
Round 2: "I'm miscalibrating." Apply to 5 better-fit roles.
Round N: Apply only where confident. Sustainable positive EV.

Employer Learning

Round 1: Vague description, 100 applicants. Below median, lose stake.
Round 2: "I lost money from too many applicants." Write clearer.
Round N: Precise descriptions, targeted pool. Beat median, earn bonus.

System Equilibrium

Over time, the mechanism drives the market toward an efficient equilibrium without any central authority judging "quality."

1
Well-Matched Candidates
Poorly calibrated candidates lose money and exit. Only genuinely well-matched candidates remain.
2
Clear Job Descriptions
Employers with vague descriptions are penalized. Clarity is rewarded with bonuses.
3
Discovered Prices
Market prices for attention and signal emerge through iteration, not guesswork.
4
Self-Calibrating Median
The platform median represents an efficient matching baseline that adjusts automatically.

The Core Principle

Miscalibration costs money. Accuracy is rewarded.
The iterated game does the rest.
